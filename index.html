About Us — Legal Helping Hands Trust

Legal Knowledge With Clarity & Confidence

Legal Helping Hands Trust is a Delhi-based charitable trust registered in June 2020 with a clear, public-interest mission: to provide free legal education to society at large.

Our Origin

The Trust was formally established in June 2020 in Delhi, at a time when access to reliable legal information became even more critical for ordinary citizens. It was founded by legally trained individuals and socially conscious volunteers who saw a gap between complex laws and the common person's understanding of their rights and remedies.

Our Mission

The core mission of Legal Helping Hands Trust is to:

  • Demystify the law for non-lawyers through simple, practical, and language-accessible content.
  • Promote legal literacy so that people can identify when their rights are affected and know where to seek help.
  • Reduce dependence on expensive legal consultations for basic queries by empowering citizens with foundational legal knowledge.
What We Do

To fulfil its aim of free legal education, the Trust focuses on:

Legal-Awareness Content

Creating easy-to-understand explanations of laws related to consumer rights, family matters, property, employment, criminal process, and everyday legal issues.

Outreach & Education

Conducting workshops, seminars, and community sessions offline and online to spread awareness about legal rights and duties.

Resource Creation

Developing scripts, videos, handouts, and digital material in Hindi and English to reach a wider audience, especially those who may not be comfortable with technical legal language.

Guidance on Legal Aid

Informing people about free legal-aid mechanisms, including Delhi State Legal Services Authority and National Legal Services Authority channels, so they know how to access formal support when needed.

Who We Serve
  • Students and young adults seeking basic legal awareness.
  • Families dealing with notices, disputes, or rights-related confusion.
  • Marginalized and financially constrained groups who cannot afford private legal advice.
  • Any citizen who wants to understand their legal rights and responsibilities in everyday life.
Our Vision

The Trust envisions a society where:

  • Legal knowledge is not a privilege but a common resource.
  • People are confident enough to ask the right questions, recognize violations, and approach the correct authorities.
  • “Access to justice” begins with access to clear, trustworthy legal information in a language people understand.

Legal Helping Hands Trust remains committed to staying non-partisan, educational, and focused on empowerment through knowledge — because an informed citizen is the first step toward a just society.
